Frequently Asked Regulatory & Consumer Credit Questions

💳 How do Fintech Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) credit facilities handle merchant dispute resolution?

Fintech BNPL lenders providing point-of-sale lines of credit are subject to federal consumer protection standards, including CFPB compliance enforcement and Regulation Z (12 C.F.R. § 1026.2). When a merchant dispute or unauthorized transaction occurs, BNPL originators are required to investigate billing errors, suspend collection efforts on disputed balances, and refrain from adverse credit reporting.

🏬 What consumer rights exist regarding customer service refund delays under CFPB regulatory frameworks?

Under CFPB supervisory guidance and Truth in Lending Act (TILA) standards, financial institutions cannot obstruct billing error resolution or cause unreasonable customer service refund delays. Origination platforms must credit merchant return balances promptly without subjecting consumers to automated rejection loops or app UI account freezes.

🏛️ How does the Administrative Procedures Act (APA) apply to CFPB supervision of BNPL lines of credit?

Under the Administrative Procedures Act (APA) (5 U.S.C. § 553(b)(A)), agency guidance and policy statements interpret statutory mandates without requiring notice-and-comment procedures. BNPL lenders extending lines of credit cannot evade statutory consumer error resolution duties by asserting APA notice exemptions.

🔒 What remedies exist when a Fintech provider freezes lines of credit during an active transaction dispute?

If a Fintech platform freezes user access or locks in-app payment functions for performing lines of credit during a merchant dispute, consumers can execute payment performance via bank BillPay, lodge formal regulatory filings with the CFPB and state Attorneys General, and publish primary evidentiary archives.
