Frequently Asked BNPL Dispute & Credit Questions

Why does Affirm continue taking payments after I returned my item?

Affirm's loan servicing ledger operates independently from merchant return docks. Until the merchant's accounting department processes the return and issues an electronic ACH settlement credit to Affirm, Affirm's automated system treats the loan as active. Under 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13, you have the right to file a formal billing error notice to suspend collection.

Why was my dispute automatically denied by an Affirm bot?

Affirm uses automated bots that query only the merchant's API. If the merchant has not updated their internal status, the bot closes the dispute for "insufficient evidence." To force human review, submit a written statutory notice citing carrier delivery tracking.

Can Affirm report a disputed return as delinquent on my credit report?

Under 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13(d)(2) and the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), lenders are prohibited from reporting accounts as delinquent while a timely, written notice of billing error is pending investigation.
